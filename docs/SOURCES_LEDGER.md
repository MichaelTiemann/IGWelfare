# Sources Ledger

A line-level record of how the cited and sourced statements in the monographs compare with primary sources. Pass date: 2026-11-09.

## 1. Purpose and method

This ledger records a time-capped pass (2026-11-09) over statements in the monographs (Documents 1A, 1B, 1C, 2, 2A, 2B, 2T and 3) that carry a citation or a sourced figure. Each statement was compared with a primary source where one could be located, using web search, publisher and agency pages, and bibliographic identifier tools. The pass began with two time-capped stages on 2026-11-09: a first pass of about 10 minutes per document slice (12 agents), then a 12-minute adversarial re-verification of the 40 contested rows plus a sample of 12 supported rows (52 re-checked, 18 verdicts changed). A third time-capped pass on 2026-11-09 (6 verifiers, about 7 minutes each, plus a skeptic re-check of contested rows) covered the remaining rows; 32 of the 35 rows previously not reached were examined in it, and 3 rows (1A:108, 1C:60 and 2A:152) remain not reached. A fourth row (3:76, Phase 1 investment and welfare figures) is set to not reached because its wording changed on `main` after the pass. Where a first-pass verdict was re-examined, the re-examination result is the one recorded here.

The pass recorded 130 statements: 128 are described by the text of `main` at c23787e and sit in the main tables below; 2 are no longer in the text and are listed separately. Coverage by verdict (main tables):

Notes on reading the tables:

- "Unverifiable" and "not reached" are statements about the pass, not about the monographs.
- Several figures in the monographs are outputs of the project's own model (for example return multiples, welfare erosion in CE terms, and required tax rates). These have no external source by construction; where the arithmetic could be reproduced from the model specification, the entry says so.
- Line numbers and statement texts are those of `main` at c23787e (8 Nov 2026); the first passes were recorded against 9fc339a and re-pinned. Of the 130 rows, 70 had the same text on the same line, 48 had the same text on a different line, and 12 had a cited paragraph that was edited: 7 kept the statement as worded (line updated), 3 were rewritten to the c23787e wording (3:76 Phase 1 investment, 3:102 phi_2, 3:217 debt-sinking and PAYE cut), and 2 (3:287) are no longer in the text and are listed separately. In the tables for Documents 1C/2/2A and 2B/2T, every row carries a file prefix (for example `2A:158`).
- Some primary sources could not be read directly (HTTP 403 responses, paywalls, rate-limited tools). Entries say when a verdict rests on secondary reporting.
- A line given as a range (for example `1B:130-132`) marks a statement whose figures or citations sit on adjacent lines. A line cell with several numbers (for example `2T:67, 96, 133`) lists every line that holds a figure of the statement. Three rows cite a different line from the one recorded at 9fc339a (1B:124 to 125, 1C:58 to 59, 2A:58 to 239) because their figures were not on the recorded line at 9fc339a or at c23787e.

This ledger is an aid for the author in locating and tightening citations. It is not a judgement of the model, its structure or its results.
