1. Purpose: Connecting 4-Year Cash Budgets to 42-Year Intergenerational Welfare

Daily fiscal debate in Aotearoa New Zealand scores policy packages almost exclusively inside a 4-year operating cash window (OBEGALx in BEFU/PREFU), whereas demographic, health, infrastructure, and human-capital dynamics unfold across a 40-to-60-year generational horizon (the New Zealand Treasury's He Tirohanga Mokopuna Long-Term Fiscal Model, LTFM, spanning 2023–2065).

This explorer evaluates any combination of 18 ongoing institutional commitments (C01–C18 Shocks & C21–C28 SS1/SS2 Pillars) simultaneously as:

  • Short-Term Fiscal Mirages vs. Trim-Tab Seeds: How retrenching early-years, clinical, R&D, civic, or Smokefree commitments books small positive 4-year cash reliefs (+NZ$7.69B across the 12 public cuts) at the cost of compounding multi-decade welfare and solvency sinkholes (-NZ$186.77B in general equilibrium)—and conversely, how modest upfront 4-year seed outlays unlock supermodular long-run dividends when flipped to +Reform.
  • Siloed Partial Equilibrium vs. Coupled General Equilibrium: How evaluating policies one ministry at a time misses cross-portfolio feedbacks across housing rents, educator purchasing power, skilled youth migration, hospital congestion, and sovereign debt-service curvature.

2. Convention-2 Institutional Commitment Algebra

Every policy configuration is represented by a signed institutional commitment vector \(\mathbf{u} = (u_1, \dots, u_{18})^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{18}\) anchored to the pre-shock 2023 economy (SS0_2023 at \(\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{0}\)):

  • Public Capability, Tenure-Neutrality, Civic & Exergy Commitments (C01–C08, C12–C17): Oriented so \(u_i > 0\) strengthens institutional capacity or tax neutrality (+Reform) and \(u_i < 0\) enacts the calibrated 2024–2026 retrenchment, deferral, privatization, or leveraged-landlord tax restoration (Shock).
  • Tax-Relief, Investor-Visa, Asset-Expensing & Defence Levers (C09–C11, C18): Oriented so \(u_i > 0\) enacts the 2024–2026 statutory tax cut, visa inflow, immediate expensing, or unfunded 3.0%-of-GDP defence uplift, while \(u_i < 0\) tightens or offsets that schedule.
Additive Vector Composition & Pass-Through Scaling: Composing any two policy bundles \(A\) and \(B\) sums their underlying commitment vectors \(\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u}_A + \mathbf{u}_B\) before resolving non-linear general-equilibrium couplings in a single pass: \[ u_i^{\text{eff}} = \begin{cases} s_{\text{core13}} \, u_i & \text{for } i \in \{1,\dots,13\} \quad (s_{\text{core13}} = 0.5915 \text{ macro announcement pass-through}), \\[4pt] s_{\text{direct}} \, u_i & \text{for } i \in \{14,\dots,18\} \quad (s_{\text{direct}} = 1.0000 \text{ direct statutory / balance-sheet wedges}). \end{cases} \]

4. Sequential Additionality Wedges vs. Order-Invariant Shapley Attribution

Let \(V(A)\) denote the Present-Value social welfare valuation of any subset of commitments \(A \subseteq S\). For the 2024–2026 Full-16 vector (\(S = \{1,\dots,16\}\)), the siloed partial-equilibrium sum is \(\sum_{i=1}^{16} V(\lbrace i \rbrace)\) = -NZD 225.61B, whereas the coupled general-equilibrium valuation is \(V(S)\) = -NZD 294.40B—a -NZ$69.06B (+30.6%) negative additionality gap (consisting of -NZ$7.42B intra-lane and -NZ$61.64B cross-lane interference).

View B presents two complementary, mathematically exact decompositions of that gap:

1. Sequential Stack Cascade (View B, Left Waterfall): Stacks active lanes in canonical order \(k_1 < k_2 < \dots < k_m\): \[ V(S) = \underbrace{\sum_{r=1}^{m} \sum_{i \in S_{k_r}} V(\{i\})}_{\text{Siloed Sum}} + \underbrace{\sum_{r=1}^{m} \Big(V(S_{k_r}) - \sum_{i \in S_{k_r}} V(\{i\})\Big)}_{\text{Wedge 1: Intra-Lane}} + \sum_{r=2}^{m} \underbrace{\Big[V\!\Big(\bigcup_{j=1}^{r} S_{k_j}\Big) - V\!\Big(\bigcup_{j=1}^{r-1} S_{k_j}\Big) - V(S_{k_r})\Big]}_{\text{Sequential Cross-Lane Wedge } r}. \]
2. Order-Invariant \(2^m\) Shapley-Owen Attribution (View B, Right Bars): Averages each lane's marginal impact across all \(m!\) entry orders—equivalently dividing every Harsanyi synergy dividend \(m(T) = \sum_{R \subseteq T} (-1)^{|T|-|R|} V\!\big(\bigcup_{r \in R} S_r\big)\) equally among the \(|T|\) participating lanes: \[ \phi_r(V) = \sum_{Q \subseteq \mathcal{L}_{\text{act}} \setminus \{r\}} \frac{|Q|!\,(m - |Q| - 1)!}{m!} \Big[ V(Q \cup \{r\}) - V(Q) \Big] = V(S_r) + \sum_{\substack{T \subseteq \mathcal{L}_{\text{act}} \\ r \in T,\, |T| \ge 2}} \frac{m(T)}{|T|}, \qquad \sum_{r=1}^{m} \phi_r(V) = V(S). \]

Why the Two Decompositions Differ Numerically

  • Eliminating First-Mover Asymmetry: In the sequential waterfall (L1 → L2 → L3 → L4), Lane 1 enters onto an empty stage and receives NZ$0.00B of cross-lane wedge while Lane 2 absorbs 100% of their bilateral Rent × ECE collision (-NZ$21.21B). Shapley splits every bilateral interaction 50/50 and every 3-way interaction 1/3 each, revealing that Lane 1 (Children, Health & Education) actually carries the largest proportional cross-economy amplification (+51.0%, from -NZ$47.57B siloed to -NZ$71.81B in GE).
  • Splitting the Quadratic Sovereign Debt Cross-Term: Along Document 3's macroeconomic welfare surface, sovereign debt crowding-out above 42% of GDP and distortionary PAYE closure above 22% are strictly convex: \[ \Delta\text{CE}_{\text{Surf}}(D_t, \tau_{l,t}) = -0.045\,\max(0, D_t - 42) - 0.00032\,\max(0, D_t - 42)^2 - 0.920\,(\tau_{l,t} - 22) - 0.038\,\max(0, \tau_{l,t} - 22)^2. \] Because \((\Delta D_{\text{retrench}} + \Delta D_{\text{tax}})^2 = \Delta D_{\text{retrench}}^2 + \Delta D_{\text{tax}}^2 + 2\,\Delta D_{\text{retrench}}\Delta D_{\text{tax}}\), stacking C09 tax cuts last (Wedge 4 = -NZ$17.34B) bills 100% of that quadratic cross-term to Lane 4, whereas Shapley (-NZ$10.73B on Lane 4) attributes half of the cross-term back to Lanes 1–3 for eroding the fiscal base simultaneously.

6. Mathematical Care in Mixed Counterfactuals: Commutativity, Two-Channel Coupling & Statutory Fiscal Closure

When evaluating mixed counterfactuals—such as enacting the constructive Generational Compact (SS1: +C21..C26, or SS2: +C21..C28) atop the 2024–2026 Coalition Shocks (hole_ss1 / hole_ss2, "digging out of a hole")—naive sequential or single-scalar approximations introduce three subtle mathematical distortions. This engine eliminates all three by construction:

  1. Strict Operator Commutativity (Zero "Last-Through-the-Door" Order Dependence): In naive sequential scripts, mixing multiplicative scalings and additive shifts with intermediate clamping causes the result to depend on commitment evaluation order—for example, applying an additive clinical overhead shift \(\Delta\bar{e}_k\) before versus after a multiplicative network cost shock \(m_k\), or clamping council-rate wedges mid-loop. Here, composePolicyBundle accumulates all multiplicative operators in a commutative product group and all additive wedges in a separate vector sum, applying domain bounds once at closure:
    \[ \bar{e}_k(\mathbf{u}) = \max\!\left(0.004,\; \bar{e}_{0,k} \prod_{i \in S} m_{i,k}(u_i) + \sum_{i \in S} \delta_{i,k}(u_i)\right), \qquad s_j(\mathbf{u}) = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(s_{0,j} \prod_{i \in S} \gamma_{i,j}(u_i),\; 0.05,\; 0.995\right). \]
    Permuting the order in which active commitments enter \(\mathbf{u}\) alters the resulting macroeconomic state by identically zero (\(0.00 \times 10^{-16}\)).
  2. Two-Channel Shock/Reform Decomposition (Eliminating Majority-Dollar Tug-of-War): Suppose an active vector \(\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}} + \mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{ref}}\) contains both negative retrenchment/deficit shocks (\(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}}\), with fast cash-window onset \(\tau_{\mathrm{shk}} \approx 4.6\text{ yr}\) and cross-lane interference \(1 + \chi_{\mathrm{neg}}\)) and constructive multi-decade investments (\(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{ref}}\), with human-capital gestation \(\tau_{\mathrm{ref}} \approx 10.1\text{ yr}\) and Topkis supermodular synergy \(1 + \chi_{\mathrm{pos}}\)). Pooling both into a single net-dollar ratio \(\vert W_{\mathrm{shk}} - W_{\mathrm{ref}} \vert / (W_{\mathrm{shk}} + W_{\mathrm{ref}})\) would artificially erase SS1's cross-lane synergy whenever a large 4-year tax shock (such as C09, NZD 14.70B) has comparable headline dollar volume. Instead, we decompose the exact coupled state vector \(R(\mathbf{u})\) into the shock channel plus the exact coupled reform residual:
    \[ \Delta Y_{\mathrm{tail}}(t;\mathbf{u}) = (1 + \chi_{\mathrm{neg}})\,R(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}})\,\Omega(t;\tau_{\mathrm{shk}}) + (1 + \chi_{\mathrm{pos}})\,\Big[R(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}} + \mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{ref}}) - R(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}})\Big]\,\Omega(t;\tau_{\mathrm{ref}}), \]
    where \(\Omega(t;\tau) = 1 - \exp(-(t - 2024)/\tau)\). Notice three exact properties: (i) when \(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{ref}} = \mathbf{0}\), it reduces identically to the pure shock model; (ii) when \(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{shk}} = \mathbf{0}\), it reduces identically to the pure constructive model; and (iii) when both are active, SS1 retains 100% of its Topkis synergy while the bracketed difference captures all genuine physical cross-terms (such as C01 landlord rent inflation deflating C21 Kaiako purchasing power until C23 cools \(Q_k\)).
  3. Statutory SS1 Fiscal Rebalance (C23) & Root-Perturbation Grazing Sensitivity: In Document 3, Phase 1 (SS1) is a ring-fenced statutory fiscal compact: enacting C23 (the 1.5% Land & 25% Estate Tax Rebalance) re-anchors the working-age revenue floor at \(\tau_l = 0.220\) from 2027 onward. Consequently, when SS1 is enacted atop the 2024 shocks (hole_ss1), C09's FY2024–2026 borrowing remains on the books as genuine sunk sovereign debt, but C23 statutorily terminates C09's post-2026 structural deficit bleed. Dynamically, this keeps the post-incubation debt-reduction slope \(\vert \dot{\Delta D}(t^{\ast}) \vert\) steep (\(\approx 1.8\text{ pp GDP/yr}\)). By first-order root-perturbation calculus, the horizontal shift in the debt-crossover year \(t^{\ast}\) caused by any secondary fiscal drag \(\delta D\) (such as C16's offshore Paris NDC liability) obeys:
    \[ \Delta t^{\ast} \approx \frac{\delta D(t^{\ast})}{\left\vert \frac{d(\Delta D)}{dt}\big\vert_{t = t^{\ast}} \right\vert}. \]
    Without statutory base closure, an uncounteracted permanent tax bleed flattens \(\vert d(\Delta D)/dt \vert\) toward zero (a shallow grazing intersection), artificially magnifying a modest 2.5 pp NDC drag into a 7-year crossover delay; with statutory SS1 base closure, both C09's sunk 2024–2026 debt (~3–4 years) and C16's NDC drag (~1–2 years) register with proportional, physically transparent timing shifts.
