## 8. Compositional Policy Algebra (Supermodular Lattice & Operator Commutators), Non-Minimum-Phase Control Dynamics, & Open Co-Design Sockets

### 8.1 Dual Mathematical Renderings of Policy Composition (`Compose_Policy_Bundle.m` & `Compute_Policy_Composition_Batch.m`)

Conventional policy scorecards (`CBAx` spreadsheets and linear fiscal trackers) treat public policies as additive scalars—assuming the effect of enacting reform bundle $`A`$ alongside reform bundle $`B`$ equals the sum of their standalone effects. In `IGWelfare`, every policy regime is parameterized over a **sparse code-indexed institutional commitment lattice** $`\mathbf{u} = (u_c)_{c \in \mathcal{C}}`$ resolved via constant-time code lookup (`CODE_TO_POS[c]` rather than brittle dense array offsets), partitioning commitments cleanly across the four Document 3 portfolio lanes while reserving unallocated code blocks for future granular splits without index drift:

* **Two-Sided Shock / Symmetric-Reform Levers (`C01`–`C18`, $`u_c \in \lbrack -1, +1 \rbrack`$):** Covers the 16 calibrated `2024–2026` domestic fiscal/retrenchment commitments (`C01`–`C16`), distressed network/grid privatization vs. Ostrom commercial-board turnaround (`C17`), and the unfunded `3.0%`-of-GDP defence posture vs. clean-exergy FX offset (`C18`).
* **Positive-Only Constructive `SS1` & `SS2` Pillars (`C21`–`C28`, $`u_c \in \lbrack 0, +1 \rbrack`$):** Unpacks the Generational Compact into its eight distinct intra-lane and cross-lane building blocks so every synergy wedge in [Document 3](Document%203.md) (`Table 19` / `Appendix Table A.1`) is individually testable:
  * **Lane 1 (*Children, Health & Education*):** `C21` (*First-2,000-Days Universal ECE & Farm-to-ECE*, $`G_{\mathrm{ECE},1}`$) and `C22` (*Adolescent Tuakana–Teina, Montessori & FYI Mentoring*, $`G_{\mathrm{ECE},2}, M_{\mathrm{mentor}}`$) — pairing `+C21` with `+C22` halts per-step unbanked Stage-2 skill fade-out ($`\delta_{\theta,\mathrm{fade}} = 0.14 w_2`$, buffering the `0.22` adolescent psychosocial/distress hazard base) and ignites **Wedge 1** (`+82.6%` intra-lane supermodular lift).
  * **Lane 2 (*Housing, KiwiSaver, Water & Civic Commons*):** `C23` (*`1.5%` Land & `25%` Estate Tax Rebalance + Statutory Working-Age Base Floor*, cooling $`Q_k`$), `C24` (*Universal Age-20 Youth Capital Grant*, $`E`$), and `C25` (*Income $`\times`$ Geography Household Bridge*, $`T_k`$, dynamically 85% retired post-2036 when paired with `C26`) — pairing `+C23` with `+C24` clears the 20% Kiyotaki LVR deposit cliff (**Wedge 2**) while expanding real *Kaiako* purchasing power in Lane 1.
  * **Lane 3 (*Science, Infrastructure & Productive Enterprise*):** `C26` (*Productive $`z_l \to z_h`$ Firm Tax Shift & Regional Food-Hub Procurement*, `1.60x–2.16x` multipliers) — matching thriving domestic youth (`C21+C22`) and de-hoarded capital (`C23+C24`) into regional $`z_h`$ enterprise builders (**Wedge 3**).
  * **Lane 4 (*Working-Age Tax, Defence & 2050 Solvency Harvest*):** `C27` (*2050 50/50 Sovereign Debt-Sinking & `1.65x` Infrastructure Pool*, $`\Delta r = -60\text{ bps}`$) and `C28` (*`SS2` `-3.75 pp` PAYE Cut to `18.25%` & Super Age-64 Kuia Care Dividend*, with `-C28` retained solely to test the Trajectory-3 `Super Age 70` austerity trap) — closing the dynastic solvency loop (**Wedge 4**, delivering `>54%` of total `SS2` welfare from the wedges).
* **Reserved Sparse Code Blocks:** `C19`–`C20` (reserved for sub-splitting `NDC` / transition shocks), `C29`–`C30` (reserved for finer `SS1`/`SS2` sub-splits such as separating PAYE relief from Super Age-64 care), and `C31`–`C39` (reserved for forestry `ETS`, agricultural emissions discounting, clean exergy, and Paris sovereign/trade risk).

`Compose_Policy_Bundle.m` and `Compute_Synergy_And_Mirror_Ledger.m` formalize why coupled reforms outperform siloed interventions through two mathematically isomorphic representations:

1. **The Economic Lattice & Exterior Wedge Rendering (Topkis Supermodularity):** Let $`W(\mathbf{u})`$ denote solved general-equilibrium lifetime Consumption-Equivalent welfare over the policy lattice. For any two orthogonal policy sub-vectors $`\mathbf{u}_A`$ and $`\mathbf{u}_B`$ (with simultaneous bundling operator $`\mathbf{u}_A \oplus \mathbf{u}_B`$), the second-order Taylor/Shapley–Owen expansion around `SS0` ($`\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{0}`$) decomposes total welfare lift into linear marginal main effects plus a bilinear **supermodular synergy tensor** $`\mathbf{H}_{AB} = \frac{\partial^2 W}{\partial \mathbf{u}_A \partial \mathbf{u}_B}`$:

$$
\Delta W\left(\mathbf{u}_A \oplus \mathbf{u}_B\right) = \Delta W\left(\mathbf{u}_A\right) + \Delta W\left(\mathbf{u}_B\right) + \underbrace{\mathbf{u}_A^{\top} \mathbf{H}_{AB} \mathbf{u}_B}_{\Lambda_{\mathrm{sym}}\left(A, B\right) > 0\ \left(\mathrm{Bundling\ Wedge}\right)}
$$

   * *Constructive vs. Destructive Sign Symmetry:* Because $`\mathbf{H}_{AB}`$ has strictly positive cross-partials across human capability (`Gate 1/2`), civic belonging (`Joiner Passport`), housing/enterprise tax rotation (`tau_a` / `Grant E`), and clean exergy (`CERF`), setting $`\mathbf{u}_A > \mathbf{0}, \mathbf{u}_B > \mathbf{0}`$ generates large **constructive synergy wedges** (`Tables 19 & 19E`). Conversely, simultaneous disinvestment ($`\mathbf{u}_A < \mathbf{0}, \mathbf{u}_B < \mathbf{0}`$—e.g., cutting community arts/mentoring *while* retrenching public water/housing upkeep) still yields $`\mathbf{u}_A^{\top} \mathbf{H}_{AB} \mathbf{u}_B > 0`$ in *compounded damage*, explaining why siloed austerity triggers nonlinear fiscal sinkholes rather than additive savings!

2. **The Quantum / Operator-Mechanics Rendering (Non-Commuting Markov Propagators & Wedge Commutators):** Let $`\mathbf{p}_t \in \Delta^{N_S}`$ (written in Dirac ket notation as $`\vert \Psi_t \rangle`$) denote the high-dimensional cross-sectional household-firm state distribution over `(a_1, h, theta_c, z, k)` pushed forward each period by the coupled Bellman-Markov transition propagator $`\hat{\mathcal{U}}(\mathbf{u}) = \exp\left(\Delta t \, \hat{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbf{u})\right)`$ (`Build_IntergenTransitionMatrix.m` and `PushForward_HH_Dist.m`), where $`\hat{\mathcal{L}}(\mathbf{u}) = \hat{\mathcal{L}}_0 + \sum_{c \in \mathcal{C}} u_c \hat{\mathcal{G}}_c`$ is the Kolmogorov forward generator over the sparse catalog $`\mathcal{C}`$. Comparing **simultaneous bundling** ($`A \oplus B`$) against **sequential staging** ($`A \prec B`$: enacting $`A`$ for window $`\Delta \tau`$ before $`B`$, versus $`B \prec A`$) via the **Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff (`BCH`) expansion** exposes the exact **sequencing wedge product** ($`\hat{\mathcal{G}}_A \wedge \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B`$):

$$
\exp\left(\Delta \tau \, \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B\right) \exp\left(\Delta \tau \, \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A\right) = \exp\left(\Delta \tau \left(\hat{\mathcal{G}}_A + \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B\right) + \frac{\left(\Delta \tau\right)^2}{2} \left\lbrack \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B, \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A \right\rbrack + \mathcal{O}\left(\left(\Delta \tau\right)^3\right)\right)
$$

$$
\left\lbrack \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A, \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B \right\rbrack \equiv \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B - \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A = 2 \left(\hat{\mathcal{G}}_A \wedge \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B\right) \neq \mathbf{0}
$$

   * *Physical & Economic Meaning of the Non-Zero Commutator:* Because the childhood skill operator $`\hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{ECE/Mentor}}`$ (`Q_h`) and the Age-20 enterprise/grant operator $`\hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{GrantE/Tax}}`$ (`Q_a`) act on non-commuting stages of the lifecycle state vector, **order of operations changes physical reality**:
     * When capability precedes capital ($`\hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{GrantE}} \hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{Mentor}} \vert \Psi_0 \rangle`$, i.e., `Gate 1/2 -> Age-20 Grant E`), mentored youth arrive at Age 20 in high-skill states (`h_4..h_7`) and convert capital endowments into `z_h` enterprise equity.
     * Reversing the sequence ($`\hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{Mentor}} \hat{\mathcal{G}}_{\mathrm{GrantE}} \vert \Psi_0 \rangle`$, or handing out universal Day-1 grants to unmentored cohorts, or imposing "fiscal austerity first, children later") projects mass onto low-absorptive states—generating the exact **`-NZD 3.8B` skill-mismatch commutator loss** quantified in the Clearinghouse ([Document 1D](Document%201D.md), [Document 1F](Document%201F.md)). Thus, **symmetric supermodularity** ($`\mathbf{H}_{AB}`$) governs *which wedges belong in the bundle*, while the **antisymmetric Lie commutator / exterior wedge** ($`\lbrack \hat{\mathcal{G}}_A, \hat{\mathcal{G}}_B \rbrack`$) governs *the optimal 13-wave launch sequence* (*Te Kāhui Waka*).

### 8.2 Two-Sided Shock-vs.-Reform Asymmetry: Five Mirror Regimes, Stern-Kick Gestation, & Behavioral Kinks (`commitment_catalog.csv` & `Plot_LTFM_Coalition_Shock_Mirage.m`)

While the quadratic Taylor approximation in Section 8.1 treats $`\Delta W(\mathbf{u})`$ as locally smooth around `SS0` ($`\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{0}`$), global policy counterfactuals are **piecewise-nonlinear and directionally asymmetric** across the origin. Liquidating, deferring, or repealing an existing institutional stock (`Shock` state `s_i = -1`) does **not** share a symmetric `-1.0x` functional form with expanding capacity or raising statutory rates above the 2023 baseline (`+Reform` state `s_i = +1`). Most starkly, when baseline National Defence expenditure (`C18`) is 1.2% of GDP (~NZD 5.4B/yr) and an unfunded alliance shock raises it by +NZD 8.0B/yr to 3.0% of GDP [Ref 59], a naive linear reflection would fabricate an impossible -NZD 8.0B/yr budget cut out of a NZD 5.4B vote. Similarly, canceling an already future-proofed hospital or rail-ferry build inflicts sunk break fees and bottleneck losses that cannot be "doubled in reverse," while hiking tobacco or income taxes above high existing New Zealand baselines encounters addiction, illicit-trade, and convex deadweight-loss kinks [Ref 6, 27].

To enforce global economic and physical defensibility across both `Plot_LTFM_Coalition_Shock_Mirage.m` and the standalone interactive workbench (`igwelfare_policy_mirage_explorer.html`), every commitment `i` in `commitment_catalog.csv` is parameterized as a **piecewise two-sided operator** mapping signed state `s_i` in `{-1 (2024 Shock), 0 (SS0_2023), +1 (+Reform)}` onto its Convention-2 dial `u_i` (where the reference 2024 shock sign in `Compose_Policy_Bundle.m` is $`d_i^{\mathrm{ref}} = +1`$ on tax-relief/visa/expensing/defence-lift dials `C09`–`C11`, `C18` and $`d_i^{\mathrm{ref}} = -1`$ on public capability/infrastructure/civic commitments `C01`–`C08`, `C12`–`C17`), 4-year fiscal/seed scale, and exponential gestation constant (`tau_i` vs. `tau_pos,i`):

$$
u_i\left(s_i\right) = \begin{cases}
  d_i^{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if } s_i = -1 \quad \left(\mathrm{Calibrated\ 2024\text{--}2026\ Shock},\ C_i^{\mathrm{shk}},\ \tau_i^{\mathrm{shk}}\right) \\
  0 & \text{if } s_i = 0 \quad \left(\mathrm{Pre\text{-}Shock\ 2023\ Baseline}\ \mathrm{SS0}\right) \\
  -m_i^{\mathrm{pos}} d_i^{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if } s_i = +1 \quad \left(\mathrm{Regime\text{-}Bounded\ +Reform},\ C_i^{\mathrm{pos}},\ \tau_i^{\mathrm{pos}}\right)
\end{cases}
$$

### 8.3 Non-Minimum-Phase Inverse-Response Dynamics, Boundary-Layer Actuators ("Trim-Tabs"), & Cash `OBEGAL` Observability Lag (`Compute_Helm_TrimTab_Ledger.m`)
